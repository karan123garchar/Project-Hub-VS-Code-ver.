Our Genesis

From Fragmented Freelancing to Sovereign Architecture.

For decades, corporate technical leaders faced a binary compromise: pay exorbitant retainers to monolithic systems integrators who deployed junior contractors, or brave chaotic freelancer platforms littered with abandoned code, dubious copyright ownership, and zero accountability.

ProjectHub was established in 2021 by veterans of Tier-1 infrastructure firms to provide a third, uncompromised path: a curated, private ecosystem that operates like an investment-grade clearinghouse for enterprise software assets and architectural talent.

“Software that powers the Fortune 500 should never be built on questionable codebases or opaque contracts. ProjectHub was crafted to embody the discretion and precision of private banking.”
